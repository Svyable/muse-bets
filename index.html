The rules

Edge bar. A leg only qualifies if model probability beats market implied probability by ≥4% — ≥6% for marquee public brands and home sides.

Concentration. Volume goes to the best slice. NFL (16-10) leads, so NFL slates build NFL-first; no-NFL slates run discovery mode across all sports.

Dogfood. 20 stack-ranked legs daily. Ranks 1–5 are staked and graded against an 80% hit-rate goal. Ranks 6–20 are paper — zero risk, pure data.

Props lane. Player props and alt lines are hunted separately for repeatably mispriced DraftKings action — never mixed into core evidence.

CLV is the scoreboard. Did we beat the closing line? Process over results, ruthlessly.

Silver thresholds. Under 50 legs = preliminary. 50+ = real rule changes. Approaches that don't earn their keep get cut.
